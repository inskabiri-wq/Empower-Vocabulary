Does anything shut down after many tries?

Hammers the real breaker with 1000 calls against a dependency that is 100% down, then brings the dependency back and checks it recovers on its own. The question being answered is whether repeated failure ever puts it into a state it cannot leave - a lockout, a stuck flag, an exception that escapes, or unbounded growth. Cooldown is compressed to 200ms so a real hour of outage fits in a few seconds; the logic is identical.
